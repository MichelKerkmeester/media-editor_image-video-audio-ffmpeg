**Run this** (set `INPUT.mp4` to your video's path):

```bash
ffmpeg -version && ffmpeg -encoders | grep libmp3lame
mkdir -p "media files/export"
ffmpeg -i "INPUT.mp4" -vn -map 0:a:0 -c:a libmp3lame -b:a 192k "media files/export/video-audio-track.mp3"
```

- **What it does:** `-vn` drops the video, and `-map 0:a:0` takes the first audio stream. `libmp3lame` at 192 kbps is a good default for universal playback. For a smaller file, use `-b:a 128k`.
- **Name:** `video-audio-track.mp3` is a placeholder. Change it to something that says what the audio is.
- **Build check:** The first line confirms ffmpeg is installed and has the MP3 encoder. If `grep` prints nothing, your build can't write MP3, so tell me and I'll give you an AAC or other alternative.
