Nothing ran here, because the Media Editor tools aren't connected in this chat. This is advice only.

**Plan:** H.264 MP4, scaled to 720p, CRF 28, with mono AAC audio at 96k. Email limits are about 25 MB for Gmail and 20 MB for Outlook, so aim for roughly 15 MB. Webinars are mostly slides and a talking head, so they usually compress well at these settings. The trade-off is slightly softer fine text on slides.

**Run this** (set `INPUT.mp4` to your file; the output name `webinar-email.mp4` is a proposal and you can change it):

```bash
ffmpeg -version
ffmpeg -encoders | grep libx264
mkdir -p "media files/export"
ffmpeg -i INPUT.mp4 -vf "scale=-2:720" -c:v libx264 -crf 28 -preset slow -c:a aac -b:a 96k -ac 1 -movflags +faststart "media files/export/webinar-email.mp4"
```

**Result lands in:** `media files/export/webinar-email.mp4`

**Check this:**
- Run `ls -lh "media files/export/webinar-email.mp4"` and confirm it is under about 20 MB.
- Play it and confirm the slide text is readable.
- If it's still too big, raise `-crf` to 30 or drop to `scale=-2:480`. Quality is what you give up.
- If the source is already below 720p, change `scale=-2:720` to your source height so it doesn't upscale.

If the webinar is long (an hour or more), 15 MB may not be reachable at readable quality. In that case a share link is a better route than an attachment. If you tell me the duration, I can work out a bitrate that fits a specific size.
