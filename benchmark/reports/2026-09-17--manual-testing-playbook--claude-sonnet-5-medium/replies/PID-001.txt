**Run this:**
```bash
ffmpeg -i input.png -q:v 5 export/001 - jpeg-conversion/output.jpg
```

- `-q:v 5` is a good general-purpose JPEG quality (2=highest/largest, 31=lowest/smallest). Use `-q:v 2` for higher fidelity, `-q:v 8` for smaller size/email.
