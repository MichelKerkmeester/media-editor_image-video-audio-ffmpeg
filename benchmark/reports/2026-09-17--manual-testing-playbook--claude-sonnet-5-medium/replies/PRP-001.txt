**Run this:**

```bash
ffmpeg -i "input/your-demo-video.mp4" -vn -c:a libmp3lame -b:a 192k "export/001 - podcast-audio/demo-audio.mp3"
```

- `-vn` drops the video stream
- `-c:a libmp3lame -b:a 192k` encodes MP3 at 192 kbps, the standard for universal podcast-feed playback
- Swap in your actual input filename/path before running

**Result lands in:** `export/001 - podcast-audio/` in the CLI runtime or your terminal
